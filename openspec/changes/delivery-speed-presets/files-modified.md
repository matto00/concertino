- `config/concertino.schema.json` — replaced flat `models` with `models.<harness>.<role>` (sparse); added `modelTiers.<harness>.{cheap,standard,capable}` and `speeds.<name>.{budgets,roleTiers,secondFinalGateSkeptic,evaluatorCleanWorktree}`; new `$defs.roleModelMap`/`$defs.tierMap`/`$defs.speed`.
