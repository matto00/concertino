## Standing Constraints

- [C1] A spec-delta change is not verified by `openspec validate` alone: run `openspec archive` against a DISPOSABLE COPY of the worktree before claiming the delta is sound. `validate` passed twice on a delta `archive` rejects outright (`archive_spec_update_failed`). Never run `archive` against the live worktree to test this. (Promoted at the design gate, round 2.)
- [C2] A `protectedPaths` glob must be validated against git pathspec *magic*, not only malformed-glob syntax. An entry beginning `!` or `:` is accepted by git, inverts or re-scopes the match, and silently disarms the guard while producing output identical to a legitimate non-match. Reject the class at config-validation time **and** fail closed in the script. (Promoted at the final gate, round 1 — skeptic-final-1.md.)
- [C3] A `protectedPaths` entry must be accepted by a POSITIVE allowlist (a fixed accept pattern for a plain relative glob), never by a denylist of specific known-bad prefixes. A denylist-shaped fix for [C2] (`entry.startsWith('!') || entry.startsWith(':')`) was itself defeated by a leading-space entry (`" !record/**"`) that started with neither character — a denylist must enumerate every evasion and is always one shape behind. Reject outright (never silently trim-and-accept) any entry differing from its own trimmed form. (Promoted at the final gate, round 2 — skeptic-final-2.md; owner granted an extra final-gate round, `extend-final-gate`.)

- [C4] A validation predicate must have ONE implementation, not independent JS and bash copies that can disagree. Bash `[[ =~ ]]` is locale-collation-aware (a `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` class accepts accented Latin letters under `LANG=en_US.UTF-8`). Delegate to the single predicate; pin `LC_ALL=C` for any residual bash character-class test. (Promoted at the final gate, round 3.)
