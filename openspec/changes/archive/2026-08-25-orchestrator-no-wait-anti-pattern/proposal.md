## Why

The orchestrator role has repeatedly ended its turn to report that it is "waiting" for a sub-agent, even in runs whose spawn brief explicitly instructed it not to (HEL-671, HEL-630, HEL-651, all on 2026-08-24/25). `core/roles/orchestrator.md` already states, in bold, at the top of its "Harness resume model" section: "Never end your turn while a sub-agent you spawned or resumed is still outstanding" — and restates this at every spawn/resume site. So the prohibition is not missing; it is **contradicted by adjacent text in the same section**. That same opening paragraph tells a top-level session: *"waiting costs nothing: your session persists and will receive the sub-agent's result whenever it arrives, however long that takes."* All three failing runs were top-level `/concertino-deliver` sessions — exactly the case that sentence licenses. It both implies an arriving signal ("will receive... whenever it arrives") and states that ending a turn to wait for one is costless, directly undermining the bolded rule two sentences earlier. A fourth restatement of the prohibition, layered on top of a doc that still contains this license, would not have prevented any of the three observed failures and is not expected to prevent a fourth.

## What Changes

- Correct the internal contradiction in `core/roles/orchestrator.md`'s "Harness resume model" section: remove or rewrite the "waiting costs nothing... will receive the sub-agent's result whenever it arrives" language so it no longer implies an automatic wake signal or license to end a turn on that basis — replace it with language that states plainly that "waiting is free" describes *session persistence* (the harness will resume this session on the same call returning), never a license to *end the turn now* while a call is outstanding.
- Add an explicit, closed enumeration of the legitimate turn-ending conditions for the orchestrator (run genuinely finished per Phase 4's "genuinely complete" definition; a decision is needed from the coordinator, raised as an explicit escalation with a stated recommendation; the CON-76 pending-escalation bubble exception), placed directly beside the corrected contradiction, contrasted against the "ending a turn merely to report a sub-agent is in progress" anti-pattern.
- Broaden the language audit beyond the terms in the ticket's own suggestion (`notif`, `report back`, etc.) to also catch the actual contradiction found: `wait`/`waiting` (bare stem), `whenever it arrives`, `will receive`, `costs nothing`, `free at the top level`, `persists`. Audit `core/roles/orchestrator.md` (the source, not a rendered copy) end-to-end and correct any further instance found, without weakening the doc's accurate mechanical explanations.
- Redefine the execution-phase demonstration so it actually discriminates fixed from unfixed behavior: the orchestrator (not the executor, who cannot observe or perform orchestrator turn behavior) must, at least once during this run's own delivery, independently establish a spawned sub-agent's terminal state from artifacts alone (report file path, `git log` commit SHA, `workflow-state.md`) and record the concrete observations, rather than merely consuming a call's return value (which every failing run also did and proves nothing).
- Require the executor's and evaluator's own reports to carry an explicit, separately-headed "what was / was not verified" section, so the final-gate skeptic has a fixed target rather than a tone judgement.
- **(Round 3 revision)** `lib/cli/render.js`'s `harnessResume` block — the Claude Code branch (the `default`/fallback `return` after the `codex`/`opencode` early returns, `case 'harnessResume':`) independently re-emits the same "waiting is free — your session persists and receives the sub-agent's result whenever it arrives" construction, verbatim in substance, into the **rendered** orchestrator agent, ~60 lines after the passage `core/roles/orchestrator.md` itself corrects. This block is interpolated into `core/roles/orchestrator.md` via `{{block:harnessResume}}` at its own line 101, the last line of the same "Harness resume model" section. This change corrects that block's copy too, in the same pass, preserving its accurate, distinct content: the blocking-call-return-value-is-authoritative explanation and the existing "poll for the artefact the sub-agent was told to produce (its report path, or a new commit on the branch)" fallback — both already correct and not to be touched beyond what's needed to remove the contradiction and avoid restating the rule twice in two different framings within one rendered section.

Confirmed during planning: only `harnessResume` contains offending language among the eight — the other seven's Claude-branch text was checked against the same term list and contains no hit that implies an automatic notification (`agentMergePermissionCheck`'s "wait for a human 'merged' confirmation" and `subagentEscalationNotify`'s "do not wait for a reply" — the latter not even reachable from the orchestrator's own interpolation set — are legitimate, accurate uses, not implying-notification language).
